# PulseFit Fitness Center - Website Sitemap

## Website Structure

The website is designed to guide visitors from learning about PulseFit's services to becoming potential members through a clear and intuitive user journey.

---

## Homepage

Purpose:
Introduce PulseFit Fitness Center, highlight its services and membership options, build trust, and encourage visitors to take action.

Sections:
- Navigation Bar
- Hero section
- Trust Section
- Services 
- Membership Plans
- Why Choose PulseFit
- Call-to-action section
- Footer 

---

## Contact / Lead Generation Page

Purpose:
Allow visitors to easily contact the gym or request more information.

Sections:
- Navigation Bar
- Contact Header
- Contact Information
- Contact Form
- Footer
